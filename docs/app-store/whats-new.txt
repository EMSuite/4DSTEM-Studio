Version 5.4.2 is a stability release.

• Origin from Bragg Peaks finds the direct beam on spot patterns whose first-order disks outshine it, such as Si/SiGe.
• Fit Ellipse on a mean pattern with sharp crystalline rings keeps the ring fit instead of an amorphous-ring model that could distort it.
• Saved disk-centre labels come back after the dataset's folder is renamed or moved.
• The run progress bar follows its count smoothly.
• EDS: Auto ID quantifies an element found by its L line on its Kα when the spectrum holds it well excited, and offers its near misses (such as carbon below 0.45 keV) as one-click additions. Long DM4 reads use less memory.
• VoiceOver reads the workspace list as buttons.

Results that change: the Bragg-peak origin on spot patterns where a disk outshines the beam, ellipse fits on crystalline mean patterns, and EDS Auto ID element lists.
